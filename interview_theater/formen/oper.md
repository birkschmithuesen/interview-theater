## Bot (EN)
Opera: a staged drama set entirely to music — vocal pieces with orchestral accompaniment, continuous or in "numbers" linked by recitative/dialogue. Time: theatrical, often musically extended. Space: purpose-built opera house (stage, pit, auditorium). Audience: passive observer, fixed seating. Text/character: libretto fully sung, fixed sung-through character. Not to confuse with operetta (spoken-dialogue dominant) or musical (popular idiom). Examples: Monteverdi's early Florentine opera (c.1600); Wagner's music drama (late 19th c.).
