## Bot (EN)
Scenic/dramatic oratorio: a sub-type of the oratorio (vocal-instrumental genre, usually sacred/historical subject) given full or semi-staged presentation instead of standard non-staged concert form. Time: concert or theatrical evening length. Space: concert hall (semi-staged) or opera stage (fully staged). Audience: spectator, not purely auditory. Text/character: narrator/chorus retained from oratorio tradition, combined with staged action, sometimes spoken roles. Not to confuse with opera or standard non-staged oratorio. Example: Honegger/Claudel, Jeanne d'Arc au bûcher (1938/1942).
