## Bot (EN)
Serata futurista: staged public event of the Italian Futurist movement (from 1909/1910): manifesto readings, poetry recitation, art display, with deliberate audience provocation. Time: single evening, touring format. Space: theatre halls, frontal stage. Audience role: targeted antagonist, incited toward riot, not passive viewer. Status of text: manifestos read live, polemical not dramatic. Not to confuse with: Dada soirée (anti-war, not nationalist). Example: first Futurist Evening, Teatro Rosetti, Trieste, 12 January 1910 (Marinetti).
