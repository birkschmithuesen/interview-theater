# szenenkern
=== SYSTEM (628 Zeichen, ~209 Token) ===
You receive one scene of a theatre project: setting, format, title, the
group's description and a numbered list of quotes the group has taken on
for this scene.

Condense it to the essentials:
- "kern": 3 to 6 short bullet points on what the scene is about and what
  happens in it -- from the group's point of view, concrete, without
  interview numbers and without reasons why a quote fits. Each point under
  160 characters.
- "zitate": the numbers of at most 5 quotes from the list that carry this
  scene most strongly, strongest first. Only numbers from the list.

Invent nothing that contradicts the group's description.
