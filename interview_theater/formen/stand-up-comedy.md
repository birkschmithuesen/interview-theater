## Bot (EN)
Stand-up comedy: entertainment by a solo performer who speaks directly to an audience, creating a semblance of spontaneity. Time: short-to-medium live set. Space: comedy club, theatre, festival, or recorded special; no props/costume. Audience: direct address, crowdwork; laughter is the real-time measure of success. Text/character: tightly crafted, memorized material delivered "as if" spontaneous; a persona of self. Not improv (genuinely unscripted); not cabaret (music/ensemble). Examples: Lenny Bruce (1960s); George Carlin / Richard Pryor (1970s).
