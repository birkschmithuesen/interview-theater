## Bot (EN)
Live action role-playing (LARP) has participants physically portray characters in real-world spaces, improvising under a shared fiction set by gamemasters; usually no separate audience. Time: real-time, hours to days; space: any environment standing in for the setting; audience role: none; status of text: fully improvised within a rule frame. Nordic Larp emphasizes collaborative, collective creation and few rules. Not to confuse with tabletop role-playing (verbal, not enacted). Canonical example: *Nordic Larp* (book, ed. Stenros & Montola, 2010).
