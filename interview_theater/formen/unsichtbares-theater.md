## Bot (EN)
Invisible Theatre, by Augusto Boal, Argentina, 1960s–70s, stages a rehearsed scene of social oppression in a real public place — a restaurant, a metro — without revealing to onlookers that it is theatre; they believe it is real. Time: real time, embedded in daily life; space: non-theatrical public space; audience role: unknowing participants; status of text: scripted but disguised as reality. Not to confuse with happenings, where the audience knows it is attending a staged event. Canonical example: Boal's 1971–76 Buenos Aires restaurant experiment on wage inequality.
