## Bot (EN)
Live radio play (Live-Hörspiel): a radio-play form performed before a live audience on stage, rather than produced in a studio. Time: single live performance, sound created in real time. Space: stage, visible sound-effects setup. Audience role: listens AND watches how sound is made. Status of text/character: scripted like a play, voiced by actors interacting live. Not to confuse with: szenische Lesung (no sound apparatus) or sound installations (no script). Canonical examples: ARD Hörspieltage, Karlsruhe (since 2004); Andreas Ammer/FM Einheit, "Apocalypse Live" (1994).
