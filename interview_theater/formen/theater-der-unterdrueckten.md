## Bot (EN)
Theatre of the Oppressed (TO), by Augusto Boal, turns spectators into "spect-actors" who analyse and physically intervene in staged scenes of oppression. Time: workshop format, open-ended; space: any room, no fixed divide; audience role: active co-author who may replace the protagonist; status of text: improvised scenes serve as rehearsal for real action. Major branch: Forum Theatre. Not to confuse with Invisible Theatre, where the audience never learns it is theatre (see [[formen/unsichtbares-theater]]). Canonical example: Boal, *Theatre of the Oppressed* (book, 1974).
