## Bot (EN)
Documentary theatre builds performances from authentic source material — records, files, letters, interviews, press footage — montaged around a political theme (Peter Weiss, 1968). Status of text: no invention, only re-arrangement; audience role: witness/judge of evidence. Explicitly partisan, not neutral. Not to confuse with verbatim theatre (specifically spoken interview transcripts as dialogue). Examples: Milo Rau/IIPM, "The Moscow Trials" (2013), "The Congo Tribunal" (2015) — real tribunal formats staged as theatre.
