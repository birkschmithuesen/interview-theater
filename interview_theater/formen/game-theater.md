## Bot (EN)
Game-theatre merges theatrical dramaturgy with game mechanics (rules, puzzles, win/lose conditions), making the audience active players. Core features: time is player-paced; space hybridizes physical/urban with virtual/networked space (mixed reality); audience has real agency; narrative emerges from rules plus player choices. Not to confuse with: live-rollenspiel-larp, immersives-theater. Canonical examples: Blast Theory & Mixed Reality Lab Nottingham, Can You See Me Now? (2001/2003); machina eX, 15.000 Gray (2011).
