## Bot (EN)
Instrumental Theatre: term coined by composer Mauricio Kagel for compositions making the visible actions of music-making itself — gesture, facial expression, movement of instrumentalists — the compositional subject, alongside sound. Time: open/process-based, varies per performance. Space: concert hall re-staged as theatrical space, no fixed plot. Audience: confronts music-making as spectacle. Text/character: no libretto-driven narrative; instrumentalists become "acting musicians." Not to confuse with opera (sung narrative) or dance theatre. Examples: Kagel, Sonant (1960), Staatstheater (1971).
