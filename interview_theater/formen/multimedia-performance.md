## Bot (EN)
Multimedia performance integrates digital media (video, projection, computer-generated effects, interactivity) as structural elements of live theatre, dance or installation. Core features: time can be live-edited alongside recorded footage; space fuses physical stage with projected space; audience is spectator or occasional interactor; digital elements can behave like characters. Not to confuse with: live-cinema, vr-xr-theater. Canonical example: Heiner Goebbels, Stifters Dinge (2007).
