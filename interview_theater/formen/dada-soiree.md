## Bot (EN)
Dada soirée: nightly variety-style programme at Zurich's Cabaret Voltaire (founded 1916, Hugo Ball) and later Galerie Dada. Time: recurring nightly events, Feb–July 1916. Space: small cabaret venue. Audience role: participant-witness in a chaotic, anti-bourgeois happening, sometimes attacking the stage. Status of text: simultaneous/phonetic poetry, manifestos, masked dance-skits — language reduced to sound ("bruitism"). Not to confuse with: Futurist serata (nationalist/pro-war vs. Dada's anti-war stance). Example: Cabaret Voltaire, Zurich, opened 5 February 1916.
