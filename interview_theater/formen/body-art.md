## Bot (EN)
Body art: the human body, often the artist's own, is the principal medium. Time: brief gestures to extended endurance pieces. Space: galleries, studios, public/street settings. Audience: ranges from passive witness to active participant. Status of text/character: no fiction — real bodily acts and risk; gender, identity, pain explored directly. Not to confuse with: popular body art (tattoos, piercing, decoration) or Viennese Actionism specifically (one bounded Vienna strand within body art). Examples: Marina Abramović, Rhythm 0 (1974); Chris Burden, Shoot (1971).
