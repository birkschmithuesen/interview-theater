## Bot (EN)
Guided-tour performance appropriates the guided-tour/guidebook format to create a disorienting encounter with place. Time: a walk, ~30–90 min. Space: real environments, freely chosen ("anywhere"). Audience: reader-walkers interpreting prompts, not passive tourists. Text/character: instructional text replaces authoritative narration; fiction and fact blend ("mythogeography"). Not to confuse with audio-walk (fixed pre-recorded track) or museum audio-guides (informational). Example: Wrights & Sites, "A Mis-Guide To Anywhere" (2006).
