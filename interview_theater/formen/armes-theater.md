## Bot (EN)
Poor theatre: concept by Ludwik Flaszen describing Jerzy Grotowski's performances from "Akropolis" onward, theorized by Grotowski in "Towards a Poor Theatre" (1968). Space: non-traditional venues, no fixed fourth wall, actor-spectator proximity designed per production. Audience role: physically repositioned to intensify a near-ritual encounter. Status: classic texts reinterpreted freely; body/voice the primary material, props multifunctional. Not to confuse with: generic minimalism — this rejects competing with film/TV. Example: Grotowski, "Akropolis" (from 1962).
