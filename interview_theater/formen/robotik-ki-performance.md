## Bot (EN)
Robotics/AI performance uses robots, prostheses or algorithmic systems as co-performers, doubles, or sole performers, replacing the human actor. Core features: time can be fully pre-programmed; space is conventional stage; audience watches a non-human performer and questions presence/liveness; the performer's status is contested when an artificial double replaces a human. Not to confuse with: vr-xr-theater, telematische-performance. Canonical examples: Stelarc, Third Hand (1980); Rimini Protokoll/Stefan Kaegi, Uncanny Valley (2018).
