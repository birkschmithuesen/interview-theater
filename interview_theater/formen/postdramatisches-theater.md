## Bot (EN)
Postdramatic theatre (Hans-Thies Lehmann, 1999) denotes theatre "after drama": forms since the late 1960s where dramatic text no longer dominates the mise-en-scène. Features: non-hierarchy of elements (text/space/time/body/media equal), "plethora" (sensory overload), "irruption of the real", shift from finished product to process/situation. Audience moves from passive spectator toward co-present participant. Not to confuse with "antidramatic" (texts/dramatists still exist within it). Examples: Robert Wilson; Forced Entertainment (Sheffield, founded 1984).
