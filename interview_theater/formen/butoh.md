## Bot (EN)
Butoh (舞踏, ankoku butō, dance of darkness) is a Japanese dance form founded 1959 by Tatsumi Hijikata and Kazuo Ohno, against Western ballet/modern dance and codified Japanese tradition (Noh). Time: extremely slow, hyper-controlled. Space: confrontational, grotesque. Audience: witness to ritual physicality. Text/character: no fixed character — body transforms via butoh-fu notation, not acting. Not to confuse with ballet's gravity-defying aesthetic — butoh is "a dialogue with the gravity" (Amagatsu). Examples: Hijikata, *Forbidden Colors / Kinjiki* (1959); Sankai Juku, *Kagemi* (2000).
