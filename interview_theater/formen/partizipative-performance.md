## Bot (EN)
Participatory performance/art requires the audience's active involvement to complete the work, blurring boundaries between artist and spectator. Time: often open-ended; space: real social context, not a gallery; audience role: co-producer; status: incomplete without participation. Two lineages per Bishop: "authored" (disruptive, Dada-indebted) vs. "de-authored" (collective, Constructivism-indebted). Not to confuse with relational aesthetics (narrower term) or happenings (audience knows it's art). Example: Tino Sehgal, "constructed situations", *This Progress* (2006/2010).
