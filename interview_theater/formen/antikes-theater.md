## Bot (EN)
Ancient Greek theatre: dramatic performances (tragedy, comedy, satyr play) staged at religious festivals honouring Dionysus (City Dionysia, 6th c. BCE Athens). Time: annual festival competition. Space: hillside amphitheatre (theatron, orchestra, skené). Audience: free citizens; free entry from mid-5th c. BCE. Text/character: scripted verse, max. 3 masked male actors plus singing/dancing chorus. Not to confuse with: commedia dell'arte (improvised, post-medieval). Examples: Aeschylus, "Oresteia" (458 BCE); Aristophanes, "The Frogs" (405 BCE).
