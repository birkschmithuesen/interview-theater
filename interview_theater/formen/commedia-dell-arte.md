## Bot (EN)
Commedia dell'arte: early professional theatre from Italy, popular across Europe 16th–18th c. Time: short runs, outdoors at fairs or touring courts. Space: temporary stages, later indoor theatres. Audience: broad popular plus aristocratic patrons. Text/character: only a scenario was written; dialogue was improvised around fixed masked stock characters (Arlecchino, Pantalone, Il Dottore). Not commedia erudita (scripted, unmasked) nor modern improv (no fixed masks). Examples: troupe I Gelosi (from 1568, actress Isabella Andreini); Scala's published scenarios (1611).
