## Bot (EN)
Operetta: a light opera in which musical numbers, songs, and dances are linked by spoken dialogue, unlike opera's sung-through structure. Time/space: theatrical stage, often smaller comic venues than grand opera houses. Audience: entertainment-oriented. Text/character: comic/satirical plots, dialogue carries narrative. Not to confuse with opera (sung-through, serious) or the musical (its 20th-century American descendant). Examples: Offenbach's Parisian opéra-bouffe (1850s); Romberg's US operettas feeding into musical comedy.
