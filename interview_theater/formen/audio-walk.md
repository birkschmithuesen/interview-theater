## Bot (EN)
An audio walk is a form in which participants, wearing headphones, follow a pre-recorded (often binaural) track along a real route, layering narrative, sound and environment. Time: fixed duration (~30–50 min). Space: a specific real route, essential to the work. Audience: individual listener-walker, guided by a disembodied voice. Text/character: pre-recorded narration, sometimes addressing the listener as "you". Not to confuse with a museum audioguide or guided-tour performance (live/text-based). Examples: Janet Cardiff, "Her Long Black Hair" (2004); Rimini Protokoll, "Remote X" (2013–).
