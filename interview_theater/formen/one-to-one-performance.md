## Bot (EN)
One-to-one performance is devised for an audience of exactly one at a time, involving close physical proximity, touch, or intimate disclosure. Time: short scheduled slots, repeated across days; space: small, enclosed; audience role: sole participant, directly addressed; status of text: minimal/improvised, built around a repeatable "score". Not to confuse with immersive theatre's embedded "one-on-one" bonus moments (e.g. Punchdrunk), brief exceptions within a group show. Canonical example: Adrian Howells, *The Pleasure of Being* (BAC One-on-One Festival, 2010).
