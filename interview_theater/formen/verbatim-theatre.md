## Bot (EN)
Verbatim theatre constructs plays entirely from the exact recorded words of interviewees — including "um"s and "ah"s. Status of text: non-fiction, word-for-word, sometimes via earphone playback so actors copy speech exactly ("recorded delivery", Alecky Blythe). Audience role: witness to real testimony re-enacted. Not to confuse with documentary theatre broadly (any document type) or plays using "poetic licence". Examples: Alecky Blythe, "London Road" (2011); Robin Soans, "Talking to Terrorists" (2005).
