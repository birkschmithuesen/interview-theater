## Bot (EN)
Tanztheater fuses dance with theatrical/everyday elements — spoken fragments, repeated gestures, associative questioning of performers instead of fixed steps. Time: long, collage-like evenings. Space: poetic, non-literal designs (water, soil, flowers). Audience: witness to raw, dreamlike material, not resolution. Text/character: performers speak as themselves. Not to confuse with ballet (structural break) or postmodern dance/Judson (rejects drama rather than amplifying it). Examples: Pina Bausch, *Le Sacre du printemps* (1975); *He Takes Her by the Hand...* (Bochum, 1978).
