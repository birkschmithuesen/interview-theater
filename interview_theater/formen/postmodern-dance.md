## Bot (EN)
Postmodern dance emerged from the Judson Dance Theater (NYC, 1962–64), rejecting virtuosity and spectacle for chance procedures, task-based structures, pedestrian movement, often by non-dancers. Time: open, repetitive. Space: not confined to the stage — streets, rooftops, museums. Audience: witness to process. Text/character: no fixed character; performers appear as themselves, doing tasks. Not to confuse with Tanztheater (amplifies drama) or Konzepttanz (its successor). Examples: Rainer, *Trio A* (1966); Brown, *Man Walking Down the Side of a Building* (1970).
