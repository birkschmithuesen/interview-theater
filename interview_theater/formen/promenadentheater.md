## Bot (EN)
Promenade theatre is a staging mode where the audience is not seated but moves through the space, following action unfolding in different locations. Time: real-time, often hours or days. Space: real environments (buildings, streets, whole towns), not a single stage. Audience: active, ambulatory, choosing paths. Text/character: multiple simultaneous scenes/threads are common. Not to confuse with immersive theatre (deeper immersion, often one-on-one/masked), though companies like Punchdrunk build on promenade form. Example: National Theatre Wales/WildWorks, "The Passion" (Port Talbot, 2011).
