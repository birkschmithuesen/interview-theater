## Bot (EN)
Hip-hop theater: theater presenting contemporary stories via hip-hop's elements — MCing/rapping, DJing, b-boying, graffiti — plus markers like spoken word, beatboxing. Time: contemporary setting, full-length runs. Space: any stage; language/relevance to the hip-hop generation defines it, not venue. Audience role: receptive, addressed in a hip-hop register. Status of text/character: scripted characters, not freestyle battling. Not to confuse with: standalone rap/battle or poetry slam. Canonical examples: Danny Hoch, Hip-Hop Theater Festival (2000); Lin-Manuel Miranda, "Hamilton" (2015).
