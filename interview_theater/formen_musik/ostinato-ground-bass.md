## Bot (EN)
An ostinato is a pattern — rhythmic, melodic, or harmonic — that repeats exactly throughout a long section or whole piece in one voice, usually without variation. A ground bass is the specific case of an ostinato placed in the bass register, forming a fixed harmonic foundation underneath continuously evolving, varying upper voices (as in Baroque passacaglia/chaconne). A performance can give musicians a short, fixed repeating pattern to hold throughout a whole scene while speakers/other musicians layer freely varying material above it — the ostinato supplies structural stability, almost like a musical "ground" the collage can always stand on. Not to confuse with theme-and-variations (there the theme itself changes each repeat; here the pattern never changes, only what's layered above it). Examples: Pachelbel's Canon in D (famous basso ostinato); any Baroque chaconne/passacaglia (Bach's Chaconne, Purcell's grounds).
