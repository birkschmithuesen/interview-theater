## Bot (EN)
A Happening is a theatrical event created by an artist, combining installation, light, sound, and spectator participation; forerunner of performance art. Time: usually single, unrepeated. Space: built environment inside/outside a gallery. Audience: invited to participate, sometimes via assigned instructions. Status of text/character: loosely scripted but non-narrative. Not to confuse with: Fluxus event scores (brief, single action) or later artist-centred Performance Art (body/action, no installed environment). Example: Allan Kaprow, 18 Happenings in 6 Parts, Reuben Gallery, New York, 1959.
