## Bot (EN)
Poetry slam: a competitive poetry performance genre combining poetry, theater, performance, and storytelling. Time: single bout, 2-3 min per poet. Space: any stage/bar/club, minimal to no set. Audience role: active judges — selected members score each poem, winners decided by points. Status of text/character: own-authored poems, no character/role, direct address. Not to confuse with: szenische Lesung (unscored, often others' texts) or Lesebühne (fixed group, no competition). Canonical example: Marc Smith, Uptown Poetry Slam, Green Mill Chicago, 1984.
