## Bot (EN)
Musical (musical comedy/theatre): a theatrical production offering music, dancing, and dialogue with a simple, sentimental, amusing plot. Time/space: Broadway/West-End proscenium stage. Audience: paying mass audience, entertainment focus. Text/character: spoken dialogue plus (post-1927) fully integrated songs and dance. Not to confuse with opera (sung-through) or operetta (direct European ancestor). Examples: Kern & Hammerstein, Show Boat (1927, first fully narrative-integrated musical); Bernstein, works of the 1950s golden age.
