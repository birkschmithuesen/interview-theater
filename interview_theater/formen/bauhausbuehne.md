## Bot (EN)
Bauhausbühne: the stage workshop of the Bauhaus school (1921–1929), led first by Lothar Schreyer (1921–1923), then Oskar Schlemmer (1923–1929). Space: rejects the classical proscenium for spatial stage situations ("Bühnenraum, keine Bühne"). Audience role: witness to abstracted, de-individuated figures. Status of text/figure: the human body treated as material; figures reduced to cubic/abstract forms via costume. Not to confuse with: ballet proper — reduced, mechanized, not expressive dance. Example: Oskar Schlemmer, "Das Triadische Ballett", premiered Stuttgart 1922.
