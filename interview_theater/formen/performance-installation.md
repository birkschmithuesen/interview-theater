## Bot (EN)
Performance installation combines installation art with live, devised performance: audiences walk through a built environment inhabited by performers, no fixed seating or fourth wall, often extreme durations (6 to 250+ hours non-stop). Audience role: free to explore and interact, sometimes influence the world; status of text/character: improvised within a curated fictional framework. Not to confuse with "immersive theatre" (SIGNA's founder distances her term from that later label). Examples: SIGNA (Copenhagen, 2001), "Die Erscheinungen der Martha Rubin" (2008); She She Pop (Berlin).
