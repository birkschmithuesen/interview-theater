## Bot (EN)
A rondo alternates a recurring main theme ("refrain", A) with contrasting episodes (B, C...), typically ABACA or ABACABA; the refrain always returns complete and in the same key, giving the audience a reliable anchor between excursions. Ritornello form is the Baroque ancestor: the recurring passage returns only in fragments and modulates through different keys, acting as a "guidepost" to the tonal journey rather than a fixed landmark. A performance can structure itself as a rondo by returning, after every interview-fragment "episode", to the same spoken or musical refrain — a phrase the audience learns to recognize and wait for. Not to confuse with simple repetition (a rondo's episodes must contrast, not just repeat) or with theme-and-variations (there the theme itself changes each time, here the refrain stays fixed). Examples: Beethoven, "Für Elise" (ABACA); Vivaldi's concertos (ritornello form).
