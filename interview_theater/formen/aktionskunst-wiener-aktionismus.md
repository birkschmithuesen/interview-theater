## Bot (EN)
Viennese Actionism: radical Vienna performance movement founded 1962 (Brus, Mühl, Nitsch, Schwarzkogler). Time: single, provocative Actions. Space: studios, public events, later museums (mumok Vienna). Audience: witness to transgressive acts; participants sometimes prosecuted. Status of text/character: no fiction — real bodily acts replace representation. Not to confuse with: Body Art generally (Actionism is one bounded strand) or Happenings. Example: Günter Brus, Vienna action (1968), prison sentence for "degrading symbols of the state".
