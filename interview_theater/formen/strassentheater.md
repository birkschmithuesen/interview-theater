## Bot (EN)
Street theatre is performance in outdoor public space (streets, squares, markets) without a fixed, ticketed audience. Time: brief, visible, to attract crowds; large works (parades, puppets) span days. Space: real public space, no stage boundary. Audience: passers-by, free to leave. Text/character: minimal dialogue, strong spectacle (puppetry, fire, procession). Not to confuse with promenade theatre (ticketed, planned route) or flashmob-intervention (surprise, no publicity). Examples: Welfare State International (UK, 1968); Royal de Luxe (France, 1979).
