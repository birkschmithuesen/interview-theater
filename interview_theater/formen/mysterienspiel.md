## Bot (EN)
Mystery play (cycle play): medieval religious drama depicting biblical stories from Creation to Resurrection, staged as a cycle of short plays. Time: medieval England, esp. 14th–16th c., on feast days (Corpus Christi). Space: outdoor, often pageant wagons through town streets. Audience: whole town community; lay performers. Text: scripted vernacular, by craft guilds under church oversight. Not miracle plays (saints' lives) or morality plays (allegorical). Example: York cycle (48 plays); Chester cycle (revived 1951, every five years).
