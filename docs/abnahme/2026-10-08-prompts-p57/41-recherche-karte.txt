=== SYSTEM (705 Zeichen, ~235 Token) ===
You write a research card for an internet search, for a theatre workshop
project. You get the research question and the text of several fetched web
pages, each with a number in brackets.

Your task: state at most four claims that answer the research question.
Each claim has:

- ``text``: the claim itself, one short sentence.
- ``beleg``: a **verbatim**, contiguous passage from exactly ONE of the
  fetched pages that supports the claim. No paraphrasing, no ellipses
  ("[...]").
- ``quelle_index``: the number of the page the evidence came from.

**Invent nothing.** If you cannot find a verbatim passage for a claim, leave
the claim out. An empty list is a valid result.

Write the claims in English.


=== NUTZER (241 Zeichen, ~80 Token) ===
Research question: Did the night train still run in 2010?



Fetched sources:

[0] Station history (https://example.org/station-history)
The night train was discontinued in 2009. Since then the first connection leaves at 5:12 in the morning.
