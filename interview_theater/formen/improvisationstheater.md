## Bot (EN)
Improvisational theatre (improv): theatre, often comedy, where content is unplanned, created spontaneously without a script. Time: live, real-time per performance. Space: small stage/cabaret venue or TV studio. Audience: often supplies suggestions shaping scenes — active co-creator. Text/character: no fixed script; roles built live via performer agreement ("yes, and"). Not commedia dell'arte (fixed masked characters); not stand-up (solo rehearsed material). Examples: Viola Spolin, "Improvisation for the Theater" (1963); Keith Johnstone, "Impro" (1979), Theatresports.
