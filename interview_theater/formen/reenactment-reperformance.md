## Bot (EN)
Reenactment/reperformance: deliberate, often authorized restaging of a historical performance work, examining ephemerality, documentation gaps, authorship. Time: restaging happens later, sometimes decades on. Space: often an art institution. Audience: witness to a work framed as a redoing, not a premiere. Status of text/character: no fiction — real documented actions re-executed, with consent from the original artist/estate. Not to confuse with: historical/military reenactment hobbyism. Example: Marina Abramović, Seven Easy Pieces, Guggenheim Museum, New York, 2005.
