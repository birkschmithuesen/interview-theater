## Bot (EN)
A flash mob is a group of strangers who assemble suddenly via digital coordination, perform a brief act, then disperse. Time: minutes (inventor: strict 10-min max). Space: ordinary public/commercial space. Audience: bystanders become witnesses; participants are performers and audience to each other. Text/character: no script beyond a simple instruction, often deliberately "pointless". Not to confuse with street theatre (planned, publicized) or art intervention (individual act). Examples: Bill Wasik, "MOB" (2003); Improv Everywhere, "Frozen Grand Central" (2008).
