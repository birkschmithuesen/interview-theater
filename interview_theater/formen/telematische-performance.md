## Bot (EN)
Telematic performance connects two or more remote locations via live video/audio links so participants share an apparent common space in real time, often producing illusory co-presence. Core features: strictly live/synchronous time; space is doubled/fused; participants are simultaneously performer and audience for each other; the live body merges perceptually with its projected image. Not to confuse with: live-cinema (single site), game-theater (adds competitive rules). Canonical example: Paul Sermon, Telematic Dreaming (1992), developed under teacher Roy Ascott.
