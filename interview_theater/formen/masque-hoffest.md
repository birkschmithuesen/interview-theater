## Bot (EN)
Masque: elaborate courtly entertainment combining music, dance, poetry and design, flourishing 16th–early 17th c. (Italy earlier, England/Scotland under the Stuarts). Time: single festive occasion. Space: purpose-built court hall (Banqueting House, Whitehall), sets by Inigo Jones. Audience: courtiers performed alongside professionals; joined final dance. Text: scripted allegorical verse (Ben Jonson), flattering the patron. Not opera; not tableau vivant (static, no dance/dialogue). Example: Jonson & Jones, "The Masque of Queens" (1609).
