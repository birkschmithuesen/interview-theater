## Bot (EN)
A Fluxus event score: brief text-based performance instruction, developed by George Brecht from 1959, central to Fluxus (founded 1962, George Maciunas). Time: a single short action, endlessly repeatable. Space: anywhere — concert hall, gallery, street, home. Audience: variable, often minimal. Status of text/character: the score is the artwork, "left as open as it could be and still have some shape"; any realization is valid. Not to confuse with: a Happening (complex built environment) or a fixed score with one correct reading. Example: George Brecht, Drip Music, 1959–62.
