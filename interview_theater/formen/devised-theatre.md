## Bot (EN)
Devised theatre (collective creation) is a method where the script/score originates from collaborative, improvisatory ensemble work rather than a single author's text. Time: lengthy rehearsal before a fixed form; status of text: emergent. Audience role: usually conventional spectator. Not to confuse with improvisational theatre (improv stays unfixed in performance). Examples: Forced Entertainment (Sheffield, 1984); Gob Squad (Nottingham/Berlin, 1994).
