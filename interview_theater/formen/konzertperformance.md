## Bot (EN)
Konzertperformance: a German theatre term for a music format with theatrical framing, distinct from English "concert performance" (opera/musical without sets). Time: single evening, often multi-hour. Space: theater/concert stage, band visible. Audience role: receptive, sometimes participatory. Status of text/character: musicians perform as themselves, as a song sequence, not a plot. Not to confuse with: English "concert performance" or Musical. Canonical examples: Julian Warner, "The History of the Federal Republic of Germany" (2020); Taylor Mac & Matt Ray, "Bark of Millions" (2024).
