## Bot (EN)
Szenische Lesung (staged reading): a low-resource theater form where performers read a text aloud rather than memorizing it, minimal/suggested staging. Time: single event, often festival-tied. Space: any venue, no required set. Audience role: receptive. Status of text/character: text is read, not memorized; characters stay gestured at. Not to confuse with: live radio play (sound design central) or poetry slam (scored). Canonical examples: Theatertreffen Stückemarkt (Berlin, since 1978); CORRECTIV revelations, Berliner Ensemble (2024).
