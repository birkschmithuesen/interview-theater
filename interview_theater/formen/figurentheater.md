## Bot (EN)
Puppetry: theatre form using manipulated inanimate figures — glove/hand, marionette (string), rod, or shadow puppets. Time: ancient (recorded 5th c. BCE Greece), practised worldwide today. Space: booth stage, fairground theatre, formal marionette house, or shadow screen. Audience: spectators; street/fairground and courtly settings alike. Text/character: puppeteer voices/mimes the figure; script fixed (opera) or improvised (Punch and Judy). Not mime/clown (human body only); not commedia (live masked actors). Examples: Punch and Judy (England, 1662); Bunraku (Japan, from 1730).
