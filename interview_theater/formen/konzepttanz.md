## Bot (EN)
Konzepttanz is a 1990s current — Jérôme Bel, Xavier Le Roy, Vera Mantero, La Ribot, Boris Charmatz — interrogating dance's own conventions rather than producing movement. Time: often slowed or stopped. Space: stage as site of analysis, not spectacle. Audience: witness to a semiotic/philosophical interrogation of what counts as dance. Text/character: performer speaks as themselves, bringing theory into the piece. Not to confuse with postmodern dance (its predecessor) or Tanztheater (retains expressive movement). Examples: Bel, *Jérôme Bel* (1995); Le Roy, *Self Unfinished* (1998).
