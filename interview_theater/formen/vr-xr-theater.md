## Bot (EN)
VR/XR-theatre stages performance within computer-simulated or augmented environments, typically via headsets or projected displays. Core features: time is often non-linear; space is simulated or layered onto the real; audience often experiences works one-to-one; the body's status is contested between physical and virtual embodiment. Not to confuse with: multimedia-performance, telematische-performance. Canonical example: Langlands & Bell, VR tour of a real location (2002).
