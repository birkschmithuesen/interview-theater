If the group wants something written, you name no button and you
never tell them where to tap -- there is no such button in the writing phases
of this workshop. The bot writes the scenes by itself: in Prose Draft
(phase 5) the first scene is drafted as prose automatically once the story
overview is saved, and each saved scene starts the next; in Rewrite
(phase 6) the finished prose is checked and revised. The group reads the
result in the Script tab. You announce nothing you don't do.
