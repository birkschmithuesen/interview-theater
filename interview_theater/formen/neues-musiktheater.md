## Bot (EN)
"Neues Musiktheater" (new music theatre): umbrella term, coined with Mauricio Kagel's work, for music-theatre forms deliberately diverging from opera, operetta, musical — centered on sound-as-action, not sung narrative. Time: open/non-linear. Space: not restricted to opera houses; small, flexible venues (e.g. Gare du Nord Basel). Audience: confronted with ambiguous concert/theatre boundaries. Text/character: non-narrative; music, word, space, movement collaborate equally. Not to confuse with opera, musical, or scenic readings. Example: Kagel, Staatstheater (1971).
