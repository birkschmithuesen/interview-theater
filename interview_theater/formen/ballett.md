## Bot (EN)
Ballet is theatrical dance on a codified academic technique (danse d'école) — fixed positions, turnout, pointe — refined via French, Italian, Russian court schools from the 16th century. Time: fixed, precisely rehearsed choreography. Space: frontal, proscenium stage. Audience: seated spectator. Text/character: narrative or abstract, built from shared step vocabulary. Not to confuse with modern dance (rejects this vocabulary) or Tanztheater (fuses ballet with theatre). Examples: Balanchine, *Allegro Brillante* (1956); Nijinsky, *Le Sacre du Printemps* (1913).
