## Bot (EN)
Lecture performance stages the public-talk format (podium, slides, Q&A) as an artwork, blurring whether the audience watches a lecture or a performance. Time: single session; space: frontal, speaker facing audience; status of text: prepared script, often re-enacting other texts/images. Not to confuse with a conventional artist talk. Examples: Robert Morris, "21.3" (1964, lip-synced to Panofsky's 1939 lecture); Rabih Mroué, "Theater with Dirty Feet" (2008).
