## Bot (EN)
Tableau vivant ("living picture"): a static, silent scene of costumed performers recreating a painting, moment, or allegory. Time: brief, often shown in sequence. Space: salon, stage, procession, or square; theatrically lit. Audience: purely spectating; historically a parlour game and charity genre. Text/character: no spoken text — performers freeze into a picture. Not masque (dance, music, dialogue); not performance-art freezes (uncodified). Examples: allegorical tableau for Henrietta Maria's Amsterdam entry (1642); Olga Desmond's "Schönheitsabende" (early 20th c.).
