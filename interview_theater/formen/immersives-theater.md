## Bot (EN)
Immersive theatre dissolves the boundary between stage and audience: spectators move freely through a built environment, choosing their own path rather than watching from fixed seats. Time is often looped; audience members are frequently masked, silent, free to roam multiple rooms. Text is secondary — meaning emerges from space and chance encounters. Not to confuse with interactive theatre, where audience presence changes the plot (in *Sleep No More* it does not). Canonical example: Punchdrunk, *Sleep No More* (London 2003, NYC 2011–2025), based on *Macbeth*.
