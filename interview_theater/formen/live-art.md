## Bot (EN)
Live Art: British umbrella term (institutionalized via LADA, founded 1999) for performance-based, experimental practices resisting fixed art-form categories. Time/space: any — gallery, theatre, forest, square, virtual event. Audience: central, from intimate encounters to mass participation. Status of text/character: no fixed script; invests in process and presence over objects. Not to confuse with: "Performing Arts" (theatre/dance as entertainment). Reference point: Live Art Development Agency (LADA), founded 1999, London.
