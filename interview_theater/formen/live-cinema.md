## Bot (EN)
Live cinema stages a film shoot in real time: actors perform before cameras on stage while an editor cuts footage onto a screen, producing a film the audience watches being made. Core features: shot-by-shot time (cuts roughly every 7 seconds), no improvisation; space splits into camera zone and screen image; audience watches the film, not the stage; text is mostly voice-over. Not to confuse with: multimedia-performance, telematische-performance. Canonical example: Katie Mitchell & Leo Warner, Waves (2006).
