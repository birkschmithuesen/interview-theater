## Bot (EN)
Social-experiment art stages a real, provocative social situation — real participants, real media formats (reality TV, voting) — as the artwork, with a genuinely open outcome, not scripted. Time: real, continuous; space: real public/media space; audience role: co-creator with real consequences; status of text: no script, only a rule-set. Not to confuse with participatory art in general (need not be antagonistic) or reality TV (stages entertainment, not critique). Canonical example: Schlingensief, *Bitte liebt Österreich* (Vienna, 2000).
