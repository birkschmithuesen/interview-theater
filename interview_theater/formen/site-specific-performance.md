## Bot (EN)
Site-specific performance is created for and bound to a particular location; removed from that site it loses most of its meaning. Time: often durational, tied to the site's own rhythms. Space: the real site, not a stage representation. Audience: frequently mobile, moving through the location. Text/character: subordinate to place, treated as material not backdrop. Not to confuse with promenade theatre (mobile audience, often any venue) or installation art (object-based, no live performer). Example: Wrights & Sites, "A Mis-Guide To Anywhere" (2006).
