## Bot (EN)
Da capo aria is a Baroque vocal form in ternary (ABA') structure: section A establishes the tonic key and main melody; section B contrasts in key, texture and mood (often more reflective, minor-mode); then the performer returns "from the head" (da capo) to repeat A — traditionally with improvised ornamentation, showing off vocal skill rather than mechanically repeating. A performance can use this directly: present a statement/scene (A), move to a contrasting, more reflective episode (B), then return to the opening material — but changed, elaborated, "sung" differently the second time, carrying the weight of everything that happened in between. Not to confuse with a rondo (which has multiple different episodes between refrain returns) — ABA has exactly one contrasting middle section and one single return. Examples: Handel, Messiah, "He Was Despised" (alto) and "The Trumpet Shall Sound" (bass), both da capo arias.
