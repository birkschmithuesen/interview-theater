## Bot (EN)
Epic theatre: dramatic form/theory, mainly developed by Bertolt Brecht, building on Erwin Piscator's staging innovations. Time: historicizing distance — events as "mass processes" with traced social causes. Space: no fixed illusionistic set; placards/projection break the frame. Audience role: addressed as critical thinker, not emotionally absorbed. Status: narrative mode replaces dramatic action; characters are social types, not individuals. Not to confuse with: Lehrstück (performed BY learners). Example: Brecht, "Kleines Organon für das Theater" (1948/49).
