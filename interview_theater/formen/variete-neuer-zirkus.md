## Bot (EN)
Variety show: successive unrelated acts (song, sketch, magic, acrobatics) introduced by a compère, descended from music hall/vaudeville (19th c.). Contemporary circus (from early 1970s): circus skills blended with narrative/choreography, rarely using animals. Time: evening programme or themed production. Space: music hall stage or circus tent/theatre. Audience: spectators; narrative in circus, none in variety. Not traditional circus (ringmaster, animals); not cabaret (more literary). Examples: Cirque du Soleil (1984); Royal Variety Performance (since 1912).
