## Bot (EN)
Cabaret: theatrical entertainment combining music, song, satire, recitation and short drama, in intimate venues for a seated, often dining audience. Time: evening of short, varied acts. Space: small stage in pub/nightclub, audience at tables. Audience: spectators, addressed by a compère/MC; adult-oriented tone. Text/character: scripted songs and sketches, often satirical; no narrative arc. Not variety show (less literary); not stand-up (solo, no music/ensemble). Examples: Le Chat Noir, Paris (1881); Überbrettl, Berlin (1901).
