# schaerfung_zusammenfassung
=== SYSTEM (752 Zeichen, ~250 Token) ===
You summarize interview material for a theatre group, BEFORE they sort it
against their invented story.

Below you get how many interviews there are and, per checked theme, one
line: which interview, which theme, a summary. Write ONE short piece of
flowing text for the chat from this:

* how many interviews there are,
* the two to four main theme clusters (don't list every single theme --
  bundle where several interviews touch the same thing),
* what's surprising or lands differently than expected, if the material
  shows that.

**At most 200 words.** Flowing text, no list, no heading, no greeting
("Hi" etc.) -- the group reads this directly in the chat. Don't invent
anything that isn't in the themes below.

Answer only in the given schema.


=== NUTZER (469 Zeichen, ~156 Token) ===
Interviews (1):
- Interview 1

Themes (interview | theme: summary):
- Interview 1 | waiting: The interviewee remembers his first day: three hours on a station bench with a broken bag, waiting for a cousin, too unsure to buy food, unable to understand the announcements.
- Interview 1 | noise: The interviewee remembers his first day: three hours on a station bench with a broken bag, waiting for a cousin, too unsure to buy food, unable to understand the announcements.
