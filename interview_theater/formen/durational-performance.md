## Bot (EN)
Durational performance: extreme duration (hours to years) is the work's content, not its container. Time: central material, set to extreme lengths (6-hour improvisation to year-long "lifeworks"). Space: gallery or public space; artist bound to self-imposed rules. Audience: often free to come and go. Status of text/character: no scripted character — real endured conditions; exhaustion becomes content. Not to confuse with: a merely long play, or a brief Happening. Examples: Tehching Hsieh, One Year Performance 1978–1979 ("Cage Piece"); Forced Entertainment, Quizoola! (Tate Modern, 2003).
