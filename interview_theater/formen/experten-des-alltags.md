## Bot (EN)
"Experts of the everyday" (Rimini Protokoll's coinage) describes casting real, non-professional people — chosen for lived expertise (air-traffic controllers, truck drivers) — to perform themselves instead of trained actors. Status of text: biographical, not fiction; audience role: witness to authentic testimony staged with play and empathy. Not to confuse with verbatim theatre (actors speaking others' words) — here the real person performs. Examples: Rimini Protokoll (Haug/Kaegi/Wetzel), productions since early 2000s.
