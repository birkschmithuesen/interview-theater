## Bot (EN)
Sound art: art using sound both as medium and as subject. Time: fixed-duration pieces (Cage's 4'33) to open-ended installations. Space: concert hall, gallery, or any site where sound and architecture interact. Audience role: attentive listener; ambient sounds become part of the work. Status of text/character: no script/character; the piece may frame silence or environmental sound. Not to confuse with: live radio play (narrative script central) or concert performance (conventional repertoire). Canonical examples: Luigi Russolo's noise machines (1913-1930); John Cage, 4'33 (1952, David Tudor).
