## Bot (EN)
Two related forms. Lehrstück ("learning play"): Brecht's radical form (1926–1933) where effect occurs IN the performers, often with no audience. Agitprop theatre: mobile, short-form revolutionary theatre from Soviet Russia (post-1918), spreading to Weimar Germany (from 1927), played by amateur worker troupes in streets, bars. Time: brief scenes. Space: found/outdoor. Audience role: agitprop mobilizes a crowd; Lehrstück collapses performer/audience into learners. Not to confuse with: epic theatre (watched by an audience). Example: Brecht's Lehrstücke (1926–33).
